How to read a candle

  • Body: where the price started (open) and ended (close) that day.
  • Wicks: the highest and lowest prices that day.
  • Green means it closed higher than it opened. Red means lower.
  • Volume bars show how many shares changed hands.
  • The average line smooths out the wiggles so you can see the trend.
