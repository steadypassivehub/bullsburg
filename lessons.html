Bullsburg · For teachers

Lesson plans and sub plans

Ten ready-to-teach, 50-minute lessons for any high school personal finance or economics class, plus three no-prep sub plans. Each lesson pairs Bullsburg with free resources from Khan Academy’s Financial Literacy course and the Next Gen Personal Finance (NGPF) curriculum.

How to use these. Print a single lesson or the whole set (each lesson prints on its own page). Bullsburg is the practice and game layer: students learn with your instruction or a Khan Academy/NGPF lesson, then practice, play a live class game, and finish with an exit ticket. Khan Academy and NGPF are free and are not affiliated with Bullsburg; check their sites for the current version of each unit.

To print, use your browser’s Print command (Ctrl+P, or ⌘P on a Mac).

Lesson 1Paycheck PlazaAbout 50 minutes

Paychecks, taxes and pay stubs

Objective. Students explain the difference between gross and net pay, name common paycheck deductions, and know what the W-4 and W-2 are for.

Warm-up5 minAsk: “Your first job pays $15 an hour for 20 hours. How much lands in your bank account?” Collect guesses; most will say $300. Tell them they will find out why it’s less.
Learn10–15 minKhan Academy Financial Literacy, Employment unit (paychecks and benefits), or the NGPF Taxes unit. Show one real pay stub and label gross pay, each deduction and net pay.
Play15–20 minPaycheck Plaza, stages 1–5, then the Tax Troll boss. Early finishers: W!SE Prep → topic “Income, careers and taxes.”
Assign in Google Classroom · Link opens straight to this activity: https://bullsburg.com/?stage=pay1
Live game8–10 minQuiz Battle, topic “Income, careers and taxes,” 5 questions. Start it from your Teacher Dashboard, class card → ▶ Quiz Battle or ▶ Market Day, on the projector.
Exit ticket3 min
  1. A pay stub shows $900 gross and $702 net. How much was deducted? Answer: $198
  2. Which form tells your employer how much tax to withhold? Answer: The W-4
  3. What does FICA pay for? Answer: Social Security and Medicare

Differentiation: turn on Beginner mode for students new to the topic, 🌎 Español for Spanish-speaking students (Money Skills lessons), and the 🔊 read-aloud button for students who benefit from audio.

Lesson 2Budget BoulevardAbout 50 minutes

Budgets, needs and wants

Objective. Students sort needs and wants, build a 50/30/20 budget from net pay, and explain opportunity cost.

Warm-up5 minShow three photos (rent, sneakers, phone). Students vote need or want and defend one answer in a sentence.
Learn10–15 minKhan Academy Financial Literacy, budgeting and saving lessons, or the NGPF Budgeting unit. Model a 50/30/20 split of $2,400 net pay on the board.
Play15–20 minBudget Boulevard, stages 1–5, then the Spending Gremlin boss.
Assign in Google Classroom · Link opens straight to this activity: https://bullsburg.com/?stage=bud1
Live game8–10 minQuiz Battle, topic “Money management and budgeting.” Start it from your Teacher Dashboard, class card → ▶ Quiz Battle or ▶ Market Day, on the projector.
Exit ticket3 min
  1. With $2,000 net pay, how much goes to savings under 50/30/20? Answer: $400
  2. Name one fixed and one variable expense. Answer: Fixed: rent or phone plan. Variable: groceries or gas.
  3. You skip a concert to work a shift. What is the opportunity cost? Answer: The concert you gave up

Differentiation: turn on Beginner mode for students new to the topic, 🌎 Español for Spanish-speaking students (Money Skills lessons), and the 🔊 read-aloud button for students who benefit from audio.

Lesson 3Bank BayAbout 50 minutes

Banking, fees and staying safe

Objective. Students compare checking and savings accounts, explain overdraft fees and FDIC/NCUA insurance, and reconcile a short statement.

Warm-up5 min“You have $20 and swipe your debit card for $25. What happens?” Discuss overdraft fees vs. a declined purchase.
Learn10–15 minKhan Academy Financial Literacy, Banking unit, or the NGPF Banking unit. Compare a bank and a credit union fee schedule side by side.
Play15–20 minBank Bay, stages 1–5, then the Fee Phantom boss.
Assign in Google Classroom · Link opens straight to this activity: https://bullsburg.com/?stage=bnk1
Live game8–10 minQuiz Battle, topic “Banking.” Start it from your Teacher Dashboard, class card → ▶ Quiz Battle or ▶ Market Day, on the projector.
Exit ticket3 min
  1. What does FDIC insurance protect, and up to how much? Answer: Bank deposits, up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank
  2. Your records say $300, the statement says $260. What should you look for? Answer: A $40 transaction you didn’t record (or an error/fraud)
  3. Who owns a credit union? Answer: Its members

Differentiation: turn on Beginner mode for students new to the topic, 🌎 Español for Spanish-speaking students (Money Skills lessons), and the 🔊 read-aloud button for students who benefit from audio.

Lesson 4Credit CanyonAbout 50 minutes

Credit cards, scores and loans

Objective. Students explain APR and minimum payments, name the biggest credit-score factors, and compare secured and unsecured loans.

Warm-up5 min“A $1,000 balance at 24% APR. How much interest per month?” Let pairs estimate, then reveal about $20.
Learn10–15 minKhan Academy Financial Literacy, consumer credit lessons, or the NGPF Types of Credit and Managing Credit units.
Play15–20 minCredit Canyon, stages 1–5, then the Debt Dragon boss.
Assign in Google Classroom · Link opens straight to this activity: https://bullsburg.com/?stage=cr1
Live game8–10 minQuiz Battle, topic “Credit and debt.” Start it from your Teacher Dashboard, class card → ▶ Quiz Battle or ▶ Market Day, on the projector.
Exit ticket3 min
  1. What matters most for a credit score? Answer: Paying on time (payment history)
  2. With a $1,000 limit, what balance keeps utilization under 30%? Answer: Under $300
  3. Why does a longer car loan cost more overall? Answer: You pay interest for more months

Differentiation: turn on Beginner mode for students new to the topic, 🌎 Español for Spanish-speaking students (Money Skills lessons), and the 🔊 read-aloud button for students who benefit from audio.

Lesson 7Stock StreetAbout 50 minutes

What stocks are and why prices move

Objective. Students explain what owning a share means and how news and earnings move prices.

Warm-up5 min“If a lemonade company sells twice as much in a heat wave, what happens to its stock?”
Learn10–15 minKhan Academy Financial Literacy, investing lessons, or the NGPF Investing unit.
Play15–20 minStock Street, stages 1–4.
Assign in Google Classroom · Link opens straight to this activity: https://bullsburg.com/?stage=s1
Live game8–10 minMarket Day, 4 rounds at 1½ minutes. Debrief with the “Talk about it” questions on the final screen. Start it from your Teacher Dashboard, class card → ▶ Quiz Battle or ▶ Market Day, on the projector.
Exit ticket3 min
  1. What do you own when you buy a share? Answer: A small piece of the company
  2. Why might a stock rise after earnings? Answer: Profits beat what investors expected
  3. Why did some Market Day prices fall on good news? Answer: The same news can help one company and hurt another

Lesson 8Stock StreetAbout 50 minutes

Diversification, index funds and time

Objective. Students explain diversification and compound growth, and why low-cost index funds suit beginners.

Warm-up5 minRule of 72: “At 8%, how long until $1,000 becomes $2,000?”
Learn10–15 minKhan Academy Financial Literacy, investing and retirement lessons, or the NGPF Investing unit.
Play15–20 minStock Street, stages 5–7 (eggs in one basket, time is your superpower, smart habits), then Practice Market.
Assign in Google Classroom · Link opens straight to this activity: https://bullsburg.com/?stage=s5
Live game8–10 minMarket Day replay: before trading, students write one rule (for example, “never more than a third in one company”) and check it at the end. Start it from your Teacher Dashboard, class card → ▶ Quiz Battle or ▶ Market Day, on the projector.
Exit ticket3 min
  1. What is diversification? Answer: Spreading money across many investments
  2. Why are index funds good for beginners? Answer: Low cost and diversified
  3. About how long to double at 6%? Answer: About 12 years

Lesson 9Real Life After 18About 50 minutes

Real Life After 18: the first-year budget

Objective. Students live a simulated first year after high school, then explain how housing, transportation and saving choices shaped their results.

Warm-up5 min“After graduation, what’s your first big money decision?” Quick-write, 2 minutes.
Learn10–15 minBrief review of take-home pay and 50/30/20. Optional pairing: the NGPF Budgeting unit or Khan Academy Housing unit.
Play15–20 minMap → 🏠 Real Life. Each student picks a path and plays all 12 months (about 20 minutes). Early finishers try a different path.
Assign in Google Classroom · Link opens straight to this activity: https://bullsburg.com/?go=life
Live game8–10 minShare-out: students post their grade and net worth; compare the paths. Which choices led to an A? Start it from your Teacher Dashboard, class card → ▶ Quiz Battle or ▶ Market Day, on the projector.
Exit ticket3 min
  1. Which two budget categories cost the most? Answer: Housing and transportation
  2. Why keep an emergency fund in savings? Answer: So surprises don’t go on a credit card
  3. What is an employer 401(k) match? Answer: Free money added when you save for retirement

Differentiation: turn on Beginner mode for students new to the topic, 🌎 Español for Spanish-speaking students (Money Skills lessons), and the 🔊 read-aloud button for students who benefit from audio.

Lesson 10W!SE PrepAbout 50 minutes

W!SE review day

Objective. Students find their weakest W!SE topic and improve it, with the pass predictor tracking progress.

Warm-up5 minStudents open W!SE Prep and write down their readiness and pass chance.
Learn10–15 minTeacher pulls the class report: the reddest topic becomes a 10-minute mini-lesson using the matching NGPF unit or Khan Academy lesson.
Play15–20 minSmart practice (15 questions aimed at each student’s weak spots), then a full practice test for students above 70% readiness.
Assign in Google Classroom · Link opens straight to this activity: https://bullsburg.com/?go=wise
Live game8–10 minQuiz Battle, all topics mixed, 10 questions. Start it from your Teacher Dashboard, class card → ▶ Quiz Battle or ▶ Market Day, on the projector.
Exit ticket3 min
  1. What is your pass chance now vs. the warm-up? Answer: Student answer
  2. Which topic improved most? Answer: Student answer
  3. One thing you’ll practice before test day? Answer: Student answer

Differentiation: turn on Beginner mode for students new to the topic, 🌎 Español for Spanish-speaking students (Money Skills lessons), and the 🔊 read-aloud button for students who benefit from audio.

Sub plans

Leave one of these with your class code. Students only need a device and a browser.

Sub plan45–50 minutesNo prep

Sub plan A: Money Skills catch-up (no teacher prep)

  1. Students sign in to Bullsburg with the class code on the board (or their saved device).
  2. Each student opens the Money Skills map and continues from their next unlocked stage. The teacher dashboard will show progress afterward.
  3. After 25 minutes, everyone switches to W!SE Prep → Smart practice (15 questions).
  4. Last 10 minutes: Study Hall to review missed questions.
  5. Exit ticket (on paper): “Name one new term you learned and use it in a sentence.”

Students who finish everything can play the Practice Market. No sign-in problems? Students can still play without an account; progress just won’t appear on the dashboard.
