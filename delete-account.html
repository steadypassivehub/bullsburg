Delete your Bullsburg account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Bullsburg: Money & Investing, made by Bullsburg LLC.

Players without a school account

You don't have an account with us. Your progress is saved only on your own device. To delete it, uninstall the app (or, on the website, clear the site's data in your browser). We hold nothing else about you.

Students with a school account: delete it in the app

  1. Open Bullsburg on the device where you're signed in to your class.
  2. Tap My class at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Tap the red Delete my account button to confirm.

Your account is deleted right away, and your device returns to the sign-in screen.

Or ask us by email

Students, parents, teachers and schools can email support@bullsburg.com with the subject “Delete my account”. Include the student's first name and last initial and the class code, or the teacher's school email address. We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done. A teacher can also permanently delete a student from the teacher dashboard.

What is deleted

  • The student's name (first name and last initial)
  • The student's class membership and random student key
  • All game progress stored with the school account (stages, stars, quiz results, streak, XP)
  • For teachers: the teacher's email address and sign-in, on request

What is kept

Nothing personal is kept. Totals that contain no names, such as a school's average score in a past tournament, may remain. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play or the App Store, not by us. To cancel a subscription, use your Google Play or App Store account settings. When a school's license ends, we delete its students' information within 90 days.

See also our privacy policy.
