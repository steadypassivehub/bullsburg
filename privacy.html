Ticker Town Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Ticker Town is a learning game about stocks and options, made by Malvolini. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app or the website.

The short version

Ticker Town does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your progress stays on your device

Your XP, stars, levels and Practice Market progress are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data) erases them.

Purchases

The full game is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, email, payment card or account details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover those payments.

Children's privacy

Ticker Town is designed to be safe for kids. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children under 13. Purchases are protected by a grown-up question inside the app, plus the purchase protections on the device.

Pretend money only

All companies, prices and news in Ticker Town are pretend. Nothing in the app is financial advice.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
